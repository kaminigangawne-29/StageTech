🎟️ Backstage Taxonomy

Every Craft. Every Credit.

Designers and operators are listed separately — because concept and live execution are distinct disciplines.

🔦

Lighting Designer

Paints the stage with photometrics, colour palettes, and cue architecture.

🎛️

Lighting Operator

Commands the console (ETC Eos, GrandMA3), tracking cues live during tech & show runs.

🎚️

Sound Designer

Builds the sonic atmosphere, surround soundscapes, Foley, and show scores.

🎧

Live Sound Mixer

Front-of-house mixing, RF wireless coordination, and live band balancing.

🎨

Art & Set Designer

3D AutoCAD scenic drafting, stage models, rotating decks, and paint finishes.

🧵

Costume Designer

Period wardrobe, fabric sourcing, distress treatments, and fast quick-changes.

📋

Stage Manager

Keeps the prompt book, calls cues, leads deck crew, and coordinates tech week.

📣

Public Relations

Theatre journalism outreach, critic previews, posters, and audience development.
